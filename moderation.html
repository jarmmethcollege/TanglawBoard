Content Moderation and Reporting

Responsible organization: Jarmmeth College, Inc. · Implemented by: Student Governing Board · Last reviewed: October 17, 2026

Scope

Tanglaw Board is designed for content published by authorized school publishers. Public chat, anonymous posting, and unmoderated student-generated content are not enabled.

Content review

Before publication, an authorized publisher should verify accuracy, audience, date, links, location disclosure, and approval to share. Draft content remains unpublished. Material should be corrected, unpublished, archived, or removed when appropriate.

Reporting

Report inaccurate, unsafe, or privacy-sensitive content to Andrei Coloyan at andreidominique.coloyan@gmail.com. Include the page link and a short description; do not include unnecessary student information.
