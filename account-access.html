Account and Access Policy

Organization: Jarmmeth College, Inc. · Implemented by: Student Governing Board · Last reviewed: October 17, 2026

Tanglaw Board accounts provide access to school information according to the user's assigned role and the database access rules.

Registration and verification

Users create accounts with an email address and must confirm that address. Email confirmation verifies control of an inbox; it does not prove affiliation with Jarmmeth College. Student access to student-only content also requires a separate membership review and an assigned role.

Roles

  • Visitors: may read content published for the public.
  • Verified students: may read student-audience content after email confirmation, membership verification, and role assignment.
  • Publishers: may manage approved announcements, events, and resources.
  • Administrators: may manage account access, membership decisions, roles, and administrative oversight.

New accounts do not receive publisher or administrator privileges. Privileged roles are assigned through the authorized administrative process and recorded in the audit history.

Account protection

Use a unique password, keep credentials private, and sign out on shared devices. Report suspected account compromise or access problems to Andrei Coloyan at andreidominique.coloyan@gmail.com.

Access changes

Access is governed by the account's active status, role assignments, email-confirmation status, and membership-verification status. Authorized administrators can update account and membership access through the administrative process.
