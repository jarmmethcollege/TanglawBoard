Privacy Notice

Responsible organization: Jarmmeth College, Inc. · Implementing organization: Student Governing Board · Data Protection Officer/contact: Andrei Coloyan, andreidominique.coloyan@gmail.com · Last reviewed: October 17, 2026

This notice describes Tanglaw Board, a development platform for school announcements, events, and learning resource links. Student access should begin only after Jarmmeth College, Inc. has reviewed the actual data flows and approved the platform and its operating policies.

Information processed

  • Account email address, authentication identifiers, email-confirmation status, and security information processed by Supabase Auth.
  • Optional display name, account identifier, access status, role assignment, membership-verification status, and the date and reviewer for a membership decision.
  • School announcements, events, and resource links, including publisher identifiers, publication state, audience, and dates.
  • Administrative audit entries for changes to roles, membership verification, account access status, and published content. Audit entries may include prior and updated values for the affected row.
  • Technical information that Supabase or the eventual website host processes to provide and secure the service, as described in their applicable service terms and privacy materials.

The current account form does not request student ID numbers, birth dates, home addresses, or user-submitted posts. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this site.

Purposes and lawful basis

Account and access information supports account administration, email confirmation, school-membership review, and access control. Content and audit information supports publishing approved school information, accountability, and service security. Jarmmeth College, Inc. determines and documents the lawful basis for each purpose under applicable privacy law before student use.

Students who are minors

The platform may be used by students under 18 only after the College has approved an age-appropriate notice, safeguarding rules, and any parent or guardian involvement required by law and school policy. Email confirmation alone does not establish school membership.

Recipients and service providers

Supabase provides the configured authentication and database services. The website hosting provider will depend on the eventual deployment. Service providers process information to provide and secure their services under their applicable terms. The College should review provider locations, subprocessors, access arrangements, and any international transfers before student use. Information is not to be disclosed to unauthorized people or used for unrelated purposes.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed to administer accounts, access, approved school content, security, and applicable school or legal requirements. The College's approved records schedule governs retention, account closure, deletion or anonymization, and backup expiry. No fixed retention period is stated here.

Security

The platform uses Supabase authentication and database row-level security policies for access control. These controls require review and testing in the deployed project. Users should protect their passwords and report suspected account compromise using the contact below.

Your privacy rights and requests

For questions or requests concerning personal information, contact the Data Protection Officer/contact listed above. The College will handle requests under applicable law and its established procedures, including identity verification where appropriate.

Questions and concerns

Contact Andrei Coloyan at andreidominique.coloyan@gmail.com. The College's applicable privacy and incident-reporting procedures govern any further review or escalation.
