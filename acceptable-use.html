Acceptable Use Policy

Organization: Jarmmeth College, Inc. · Implemented by: Student Governing Board · Last reviewed: October 17, 2026

Permitted use

Use Tanglaw Board to view school information and educational resources relevant to the service's purpose.

Prohibited conduct

  • Attempting to bypass access controls, impersonate another user, or obtain another person's private data.
  • Submitting or linking to material that is unlawful, harassing, discriminatory, deceptive, unsafe, or unauthorized.
  • Sharing private student information or confidential school information without authorization.
  • Using automated tools to disrupt or scrape the service without written approval.
